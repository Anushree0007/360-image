360° Interactive Panorama Frame

Rendered with high-performance Vanilla WebGL. Click and drag with mouse to look around in 360°, scroll wheel to zoom.

800 × 480 px
Drag mouse to explore • Scroll to zoom

How to use this on your Figma Webpage:

  • In Figma Design: Create a frame with your desired width and height (e.g., 800px × 480px).
  • In Figma Prototype: Insert the free "Iframe" or "Webpage Embedder" plugin and paste the hosted link of this page.
  • On your Published Webpage (HTML / Webflow / Framer): Simply add a <div class="panorama-frame"> with your specific frame width/height, and the WebGL canvas fills the frame perfectly!
  • Zero Dependencies: No heavy libraries needed. It runs smoothly on all mobile and desktop devices.
